Privacy Policy for Nav2 Mission Planner
Introduction
Nav2 Mission Planner is a ROS2-based navigation and mission planning application designed for robotics applications. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.
Data Collection
Nav2 Mission Planner does not collect any personal information from its users. The application operates entirely on your device and the robots you connect to.
Information Usage
The application stores the following data locally on your device:
Map data for navigation
Bookmarks and waypoints you create
Robot connection settings
Application preferences and configurations
This information is stored locally to provide functionality and is not transmitted to our servers.
ROS2 Connections
When you connect to a ROS2-enabled robot:
The application establishes a connection to your robot using the connection details you provide
Data is exchanged directly between your device and your robot
We do not intercept, store, or have access to this communication
Third-Party Services
Nav2 Mission Planner does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
Data Storage
All application data is stored locally on your device. You can clear this data by clearing the application data through your device settings.
Permissions
The application may request the following permissions:
Storage: To save maps and mission data
Camera/Photos: For capturing and saving images during missions
Network: To connect with your robot
Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy as we add new features. Please review this policy periodically.
